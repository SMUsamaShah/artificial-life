Rule and verification

Each of 3,600 particles counts its nearby neighbors on the left and right, turns, then moves forward. The same rule runs from randomly scattered positions and headings at every step.

The observer tracks groups by shared particle IDs. It records a division once both daughters persist and their tracked particles remain beyond sensing range. Replays advance the same random start to the selected step.
