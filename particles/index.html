Rules and verification

Each particle counts neighbours on the left and right, then turns and moves. Two speeds mixes slow and fast particles. In Chase & evade, each type also steers towards or away from nearby particles of the other type.

Behaviour and arrangement restart the chosen seed. Lineage replays use Classic and Scatter. The observer tracks divisions by following particle IDs and checking whether the daughters persist apart.

Uncapped runs the simulation continuously and samples the results for display. Replay clips pause at the end of their recorded interval.
